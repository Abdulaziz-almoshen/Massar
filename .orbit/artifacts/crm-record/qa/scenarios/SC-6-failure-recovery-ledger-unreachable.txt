FINDING QA-1 (P1) — RAISED at 388832f, RE-VERIFIED RESOLVED at f54835c.
At 388832f the three retries above were three 503s: `db.connected` was a one-way latch, set
true only in init() and false by the pool 'error' event, never re-tested — so «أعد المحاولة»
was an instruction the system could not honour until the PROCESS restarted.
At this commit `upsertProps` calls `reprobe()`, which re-tests the pool with SELECT 1 before
refusing a human's typed fact, and `propsWritable` is `db.enabled()` alone so a transient blip
no longer greys out every editor. The three 200s printed above are the measurement, on the same
PID; the isolated failover run is SC-7. The engine log states the recovery rather than hiding it:
  {"at":"db","msg":"reconnected after a pool error"}

VERDICT: PASS — AC-8 holds, and the recovery the panel promises now works (QA-1 resolved).
