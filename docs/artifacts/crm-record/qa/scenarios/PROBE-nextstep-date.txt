FINDING QA-5 (P2, PRE-EXISTING at 388832f, NOT introduced by f54835c).
FR-4 and design-plan §3 field 4 describe الخطوة التالية as «short text + optional date», and
NFR-1 bounds that date — but the panel has no date control, so a human can neither set a date
nor correct a wrong one; only the agent's parse (contact.scheduledAt) is ever displayed.
Consequence for the QA-3 fix: `bad_date` is correct and proven at the route (SC-8 §2), and the
panel owns a sentence for it (dashboard.ts:2704), but no operator action can currently produce
that code. The fix is right; half of it is unreachable until the date control exists.
No data is lost or misreported — the displayed date falls back to contact.scheduledAt — so this
is P2, and it belongs in the Planner's backlog, not in this cycle's fix list.
