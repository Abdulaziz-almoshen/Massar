── ruling ──

  Repair it: make the success message state the SHAPE checked rather than the PROPERTY hoped
  for; fix blankFormatted's left boundary; add the literal-digit-in-an-Arabic-string rule and

  the only place it cannot be fooled by syntax, and the only scope that grows by itself.
