# مسار archive — recovered 2026-09-04

Extracted from `_مسار.zip` (Aug 10 2026) during the /office-hours session that produced
`docs/designs/massar-vision-ux-and-pitch.md`.

These two documents are the best requirements this project has, and three design documents were
written without them. They are kept here because they contain facts the engine contradicts:

- **`مراحل-البيع-ونتائج-المراحل.md`** — eight sales stages WITH probability weights, a dependent
  outcome/reason taxonomy per stage (~28 values), and the department-routing fix for the two
  stages that stall longest. Its section 4 is a **Zoho** implementation plan that never went live.
- **`موجز-تصميم-نظام-المبيعات.md`** — four roles including sector managers, and a targets spine
  (annual per product, quarterly split, RAG at 70/50) that the engine has no concept of.

Not copied here: the binary assets from the archive (screenshots, a Seha Business dashboard, the
design canvas). Both repos are public — keep customer-identifying material out of them.

**Status of the original effort (confirmed 2026-09-04):** Zoho was never deployed, and the source
document was lost inside Lean. These two files are the only surviving record of Lean's own sales
process definition — which is why they are committed here rather than left in a zip. Recovering
them may be the most durable thing this session produced.

**Design-system note:** the brief specifies navy + gold + Tajawal. `DESIGN.md` specifies neutral
greys + a single teal accent + Cairo, and a design review removed the last navy and gold from the
product on 2026-09-03. **DESIGN.md wins.** Do not reintroduce the brief's palette.
