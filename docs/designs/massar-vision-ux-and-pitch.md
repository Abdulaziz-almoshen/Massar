## Why this session happened

*"I'm not happy with the CRM, how we track potentially all of this. Not as user experience, not as
functionality, not as the vision."*

That reaction was correct, and the reviews had already diagnosed it: **twenty-one database tables
and zero screens.** Massar was designed as a data model wearing a product plan's clothes. The
44-week plan put 19 weeks into rebuilding CRM commodity and 3 weeks into the only thing nobody
sells.

Then the archive changed the diagnosis again.

## What the archive changed

`_مسار.zip` contained three documents that had never entered this project's design record.
Everything below is from the founder's own files.

**1. A complete sales-system brief exists** (`موجز-تصميم-نظام-المبيعات.md`) describing a product
Massar has never been. Four roles, not two: موظف المبيعات · مدير المنتج · **مدير القطاع** ·
المدير التنفيذي, with a drill path تنفيذي ← قطاع ← منتج ← فرصة ← عميل. Its spine is **targets**:
annual per product, split quarterly, achieved against target, RAG-coloured at 70% and 50%.

> **Massar has no concept of a target anywhere in 15,780 lines.** That is what an executive buys,
> and it is entirely absent.

**2. The stage model in the engine is wrong.** `مراحل-البيع-ونتائج-المراحل.md` defines eight
stages carrying probability weights:

| المرحلة | الوزن | معيار الانتقال |
|---|---|---|
| تواصل أولي | 10% | وصلنا لصاحب القرار وأبدى اهتمامًا مبدئيًا |
| اكتشاف الحاجة | 25% | تأكدت الحاجة والحجم وصاحب القرار والميزانية |
| عرض المنتج | 45% | تم تقديم المنتج وقبول العميل للقيمة |
| التقييم التقني | 65% | اجتاز التكامل مع صحة/صحتي ومتطلبات الأمن |
| عرض السعر | 80% | عرض سعر مقبول مبدئيًا |
| التفاوض والاعتماد | 90% | توافق على الشروط ودخول التعاقد |
| إغلاق – ربح | 100% | تم التوقيع |
| إغلاق – خسارة | 0% | اعتذر العميل (سجّل السبب) |

The engine ships `contact | present | tech | negotiate | won | lost`. No qualification stage, no
quote stage, **and no weights — so no weighted forecast is computable.** A forecast is the number
a sales director is measured on.

Each stage also carries a dependent **نتيجة المرحلة** and **سبب النتيجة**, each mapped to a
prescribed action. Roughly 28 outcomes. This is Lean's actual operating logic and none of it is
modelled.

**3. The sentence that is the whole product:**

> المرحلتان **التقييم التقني** و**التفاوض والاعتماد** هما الأطول تعثّرًا … تُفلتر الإجراءات
> المعلّقة حسب الإدارة، ما يعالج تعثّر مرحلتي التقييم التقني والتفاوض **حيث تنتقل المسؤولية بين
> الإدارات**.

Deals do not stall because nobody tracked them. They stall **when responsibility crosses a
department boundary** — إدارة المنتج · المبيعات · التقنية · القانونية · المشتريات · أمن المعلومات
— and nobody can see whose desk it is on. The document's prescribed fix is one field:
**الإدارة المسؤولة** on every action, so pending work filters by department.

That is what the founder asked for in the first session, in his own words: a CRM *between* product
and sales. The 44-week plan modelled it as a one-time list transfer instead of a per-stage,
per-department obligation.

**4. There was an implementation plan for Zoho — and it is dead.** Section 4 of the stage document
is titled «التطبيق في Zoho» with dependent-picklist configuration, activity fields, colour badges
and four canned reports. **Confirmed by the founder on 2026-09-04: Zoho is not live, and the
document itself was lost inside Lean.**

That is the most important fact in this document, and it cuts two ways.

**In your favour:** there is no vendor competing for this. The pitch is clean. And you are not
proposing something new — you are **finishing something Lean already started and lost**, which is
a far easier thing to fund than a novel idea. You can walk in holding work that was blessed once
already.

**Against you:** somebody at Lean designed this properly — eight stages, an outcome taxonomy,
department routing, targets — and it produced no product and then vanished. **Massar is the third
attempt at a Lean sales system.** The prior one did not fail for lack of a good design; the design
is excellent. It failed for some other reason, and that reason is still in the building.

## Problem Statement

Lean sells several products through one shared sales team. A deal's momentum dies at the two
stages where ownership leaves the salesperson — التقييم التقني and التفاوض والاعتماد — because
responsibility moves to technical, legal, procurement or infosec, and no system shows whose desk
the deal is sitting on. Meanwhile every product manager who hands over leads has no way to learn
what happened to them, and the record itself is reconstructed from memory into a shared sheet days
after the conversation.

## Demand Evidence

**Stated honestly: there is still none from a user.** Across 25 days, every stakeholder quote in
this project is the founder's. No rep has used Massar unaided. No lost deal has been quantified.
The morning design record says it plainly: *"sponsor buy-in, not user demand"* and *"Who is the
first rep, by name? Nobody was named."*

What the archive adds is **institutional** evidence, which is weaker than user evidence but real:
someone at Lean cared enough to write an eight-stage model with outcome taxonomies and a Zoho
configuration plan. That document is proof the problem is recognised. It is not proof anyone will
change their Tuesday.

## Status Quo

A rep finishes a call and records **nothing immediately**. It reaches the shared Google Sheet
later — end of day or end of week — reconstructed from memory, and mostly only for deals already
going well. The sheet is free, shared, familiar, and already open. Alongside it sits the Zoho
plan, unbuilt.

## Target User & Wedge

**User: the sales rep.** Buyer: the sales director. Where they conflict, the rep's screen wins — a
tool a director mandates and a rep resents gets opened once.

The wedge is four screens, in this order:

1. **«ما يستحق اتصالك اليوم»** — the rep's day, ranked by earned signal, one tap per outcome, a
   drafted Arabic follow-up returned immediately. Phone-first.
2. **ملف الجهة — "it already knows"** — open a clinic and the conversation is already read:
   seriousness with its evidence, days of silence, what they asked about, the next step. Nobody
   typed it.
3. **المستهدفات** — target versus achieved, quarterly, RAG at 70/50, drilling قطاع ← منتج ← مندوب.
4. **«أين تتعثّر الصفقات»** — pending actions grouped by الإدارة المسؤولة. The screen Zoho's
   defaults do not give you and the reason this product exists.

## Premises (all confirmed)

1. Buyer is the director, user is the rep, **the rep's screen wins** conflicts.
2. **Massar writes to the sheet before it asks anyone to leave it.** Adoption without migration.
3. **Zero typing is the promise**, not a feature. Any screen that asks a rep to type has lost.
4. The defensible asset is **reading**, not storing — with one correction made during the session:
   the **targets spine is not commodity**. It is Lean-specific, it is what the executive buys, and
   neither Zoho's defaults nor Frappe hand it to you.
5. The demo runs on **real conversations**, so a scrubbed demo account is a prerequisite. Both
   repos are public.
6. **Massar is the only attempt standing.** Originally framed as "replaces the Zoho plan"; the
   founder confirmed Zoho is not live and the source document was lost. The obligation is
   unchanged and now sharper — Massar must carry targets, the eight weighted stages and
   الإدارة المسؤولة before it is pitched, because it is inheriting a design Lean already agreed
   with once, not competing with one.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Demo first, then build — RECOMMENDED, NOT CHOSEN
Two weeks (human) / 2-3 days (CC). Scrubbed demo account, the four screens seeded honestly,
labelled a prototype. Pitch, get a named sponsor and a named rep, then build.
**Why it was recommended:** 25 days and 15,780 lines have produced zero conversations with a rep.
A director's yes IS the missing evidence and it costs two weeks instead of eight.
**Why the founder declined it:** a director in that building who has been shown vapourware before
will discount a prototype. That is domain knowledge this document cannot overrule.

### Approach B: Build the spine for real, then pitch — CHOSEN
Six to eight weeks (human) / 2-3 weeks (CC). Targets, the eight weighted stages and
الإدارة المسؤولة land in the live engine on real Lean data. The pitch runs on a working system.

### Approach C: Frappe for the spine, Massar for the moat — not chosen
Four to five weeks. Frappe CRM (AGPL-3.0, 3.5k stars, unlimited users, free) supplies the deal
page, kanban, custom views and a WhatsApp integration — sections 5.4 to 5.7 of the founder's own
brief. Massar builds only the targets rollup, the department-routed outcomes and the Arabic
reading layer. Lean has already hand-ported Frappe's information architecture once
(`docs/artifacts/frappe-port/`).
**Gating unknown, unverified:** Frappe's Arabic RTL quality. Half a day to check. Not claimed
either way here.

## Recommended Approach

Approach B, as chosen, with one condition attached: **the adoption risk of B is the risk that
killed the three previous versions of this plan.** The mitigation is Gate A from the amended
platform plan, pulled forward — the rep queue and the outcome view ship in the first three weeks
of the eight, and one named rep uses them before the remaining five weeks are spent.

Build order:

| # | What | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The eight stages with weights, replacing the six | Everything else reads from stage. A forecast is impossible without weights |
| 2 | `الإدارة المسؤولة` + `الشخص المسؤول` on every engagement | One field. It is the differentiator and it is nearly free |
| 3 | Targets: product × year × quarter, achieved computed, sector rollup | What the executive buys. Massar has none of it |
| 4 | The rep's day + the account record | The moat, already 80% built (`signal-domain.ts`) |
| 5 | Sheet write-back | Adoption without migration |
| 6 | نتيجة المرحلة + سبب النتيجة dependent taxonomies | ~28 values. Rich, but useless before 1-4 exist |

## Constraints

- Arabic-first, RTL, on a phone for the rep.
- **No WhatsApp send to any number.** Standing rule; the pitch demo cannot send.
- `dashboard.ts` is 4,127 lines under ADR-0001 anchored edits. New screens get their own
  `*-crm.ts` module.
- **A design-system conflict must be resolved before any screen is drawn.** The archive's brief
  specifies كحلي عميق + ذهبي هادئ + Tajawal. `DESIGN.md` specifies pure-neutral greys + a single
  teal accent + Cairo — and a design review yesterday deliberately *removed* the last navy and
  gold from the product. **`DESIGN.md` wins; the brief's palette is superseded.** Recorded so
  nobody reintroduces gold from a document that looks authoritative.

## Accessibility (acceptance criteria, not aspiration)

Keyboard traversal with visible focus · logical focus order in RTL · focus trap and restore on
drawers · screen-reader announcements on save and failure · **status never encoded by colour
alone** (the RAG badges in the brief violate this as specified — pair every colour with a label) ·
reduced motion honoured · 200% reflow · 44px minimum targets · tested at 360/390/430 CSS px.

Arabic correctness: Arabic-Indic numerals for quantities, **Latin `dir="ltr"` monospace for
machine identifiers**; `pluralizeArabic` for every count (`dashboard.ts` calls it zero times
today); `<bdi>` for mixed Latin (zero in the codebase today); calendar pinned explicitly; SLA
arithmetic anchored to Asia/Riyadh.

## Success Criteria

Behavioural, not functional. Each phase carries one number a sales manager would repeat:

- One named rep records **three or more engagements on a median working day**, three weeks running.
- **Half or more** of the calls a rep says they made appear within 24 hours.
- Median days from handoff to first contact, **measured before and after**.
- Pending actions by department: the count of deals stalled >14 days in التقييم التقني or
  التفاوض والاعتماد, trending down.
- Forecast exists at all: weighted pipeline value per product against target.

## Open Questions

1. **RESOLVED (2026-09-04):** Zoho is not live and the source document was lost. No vendor
   competition.
2. **Why did the previous attempt die?** This replaces the Zoho question as the highest-value
   unknown in this document. The prior design was good and it produced nothing. Until you know
   whether it died because the author left, because sales would not adopt it, because no one owned
   it, or because a reorg ate it, **Massar has no defence against dying the same way** — and good
   software prevents none of those four causes.
3. **Which sectors and departments exist?** `الهيكل-التنظيمي.html` is an empty form. The targets
   rollup cannot be built without the real hierarchy.
4. Are targets set per product, per rep, or both? The brief implies per product; commission
   usually implies per rep.
5. Frappe's Arabic RTL quality — gates Approach C if B stalls.
6. What is `Seha_Business_v13` and does Massar need to sell it, report on it, or ignore it?

## The Assignment

**Get the three numbers, this week.** Reconstruct the last three handoffs by hand: how many
accounts were never contacted, the median days from handoff to first contact, and how many reached
a real conversation. Half a day of manual work. It has sat unclaimed in every design document for
25 days.

Without them you walk into the pitch with a product and no problem statement the director
recognises as theirs. With them, your opening line is a question they cannot answer about their
own team — which is the strongest opening available and the one thing no prototype can fake.

**Second, and it is one conversation:** find whoever wrote the stage document and ask why it went
nowhere. Not to assign blame — to learn which of the four failure modes is waiting for you. That
answer shapes the pitch more than any screen does, because the objection in the room will not be
"does it work." It will be the unspoken "we tried this before."
