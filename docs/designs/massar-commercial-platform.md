## Problem Statement

Lean's product and sales departments run one commercial engine on a shared spreadsheet. Massar is
meant to replace that sheet with one platform: WhatsApp campaigns to prospect clinics with an Arabic
AI salesperson that reports who is serious, plus lead management across every channel (campaign,
call, visit, referral, inbound) that both departments work from. In the founder's words: "this is a
commercial product platform kinda like crm."

## Demand Evidence

- Founder's claim: Lean's sales/commercial lead "changed behaviour or paid". Pushed twice for the
  specific costly action; none was named. Recorded as **sponsor buy-in, not user demand**.
- The engine has run real Arabic conversations with clinics during campaign tests, but the founder
  set the stage at **pre-product, no real users yet**: nobody at Lean has moved their week into it.
- No rep has been observed working from Massar without help. No lost deal has been quantified.
- Verdict: demand is a hypothesis held by the CEO. The assignment below is how it becomes evidence.

## Status Quo

Product and sales share leads in a **shared spreadsheet / Google Sheet**. It is free, familiar, both
departments already open it, and it does not write itself. The real competitor is that sheet, not
Wati, CM, or HubSpot. The founder could not yet name the last deal the sheet cost.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

- **Buyer:** the CEO (the founder), who wants one truth on pipeline across departments.
- **First user:** an unnamed sales rep. Massar wins only if the rep types nothing.
- **Narrowest wedge (declined as the whole scope, kept as the first slice):** a rep work queue on the
  existing engine, ranked by earned signals, with one-tap outcomes and a sheet import.
- **Chosen scope:** the full commercial platform (Approach B), sequenced so the wedge ships first.

## Constraints

- No WhatsApp send to any number without an explicit allowlist from the founder (standing rule).
- Gupshup sandbox number today; production WABA migration is its own project.
- Business rules live in pure `*-domain.ts` modules with tests; no model-produced numbers on the record.
- Arabic MSA, RTL, the approved Frappe-derived visual system in `DESIGN.md` and `design/approved.json`.
- New screens are anchored edits into `src/dashboard.ts` under ADR-0001; no range edits.
- Both repos are public; every push is a publication.

## Premises (agreed)

1. The real competitor is the shared spreadsheet, not Wati or HubSpot.
2. The buyer is the CEO; the first user is a sales rep; the rep adopts only if Massar costs zero typing.
3. Arabic WhatsApp lead scoring is a commodity in the GCC (E8 Engage, SalesAgentAI, Kipps, Comniq).
   Massar's edge is the **self-writing record**: facts with provenance and signals earned from the
   ledger, not the chatbot.
4. **Revised after cross-model challenge:** calls and visits are captured only after a
   20-opportunity audit shows off-WhatsApp interactions change stage, and then never as forms
   (call log, calendar, Arabic voice note, or one tap).
5. Doing nothing keeps the sheet; the cost is leads product qualified that sales never worked. Not
   yet quantified.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Rep work queue + sheet import (recommended by Claude and Codex; kept as slice 1)
One screen: today's queue, one-tap outcomes, xlsx/CSV import of the shared sheet. Effort M (six
pieces, listed in the contract below). Risk Low. Reuses ledger, `signal-domain.ts`, `opps-domain.ts`,
facts, board, audience import. Declined by the founder as the whole scope; adopted as the first
slice of B.

### Approach B: Full commercial platform (chosen)
Multi-channel lead capture, product ↔ sales handoff, reports, roles, the CRM vision end to end.
Effort XL. Risk High. Pros: matches the founder's vision; one truth for the CEO. Cons: every manual
channel is a form the rep must fill, which is how the sheet wins back; no adoption evidence until
the first slice is in a rep's hands.

### Approach C: Write back into the Google Sheet (declined)
Massar adds seriousness, last reply, and next action as sheet columns. Effort S. Cheapest demand
test, but Massar becomes a plugin to the competitor and nothing self-writes. Declined.

## Recommended Approach

**Founder's decision: Approach B, the full commercial platform.** Claude and Codex both recommended
A. The founder chose B knowing the trade: the vision end to end, at the cost of adoption evidence
arriving late. This supersedes CLAUDE.md §1 goal 3 (a separate Next.js dashboard): the UI stays on
`src/dashboard.ts` until adoption is proven, and CLAUDE.md is updated when this doc is accepted.

To keep B from failing the way top-down CRMs fail, these rules bind the build:

- **Zero-typing is a hard rule for channel entries.** How a lead or an interaction gets in is one
  tap, an import, a voice note, or automatic. The existing opportunity create form (`opps-crm.ts`)
  is exempt until the import replaces it.
- **Slice A ships first**, because it is the smallest part of B that produces the adoption
  measurement. Its contract is below.
- **Every later B component has an entry gate:**
  - Calls and visits: after the 20-opportunity audit, in the shape the audit shows (premise 4).
  - Product ↔ sales handoff: defined now as one event, a product-qualified lead enters the queue
    at stage `contact` with source `referral` and the product person's name as provenance; built
    after slice A shows at least one rep adopted.
  - Reports for the CEO: after slice A adoption is non-zero; they read the ledger and the
    tap-stored stage, never a hand-reconciled number.
  - Roles and permissions: after a second rep is on the pilot; before that, one login.
  - A live Google Sheets connector (instead of xlsx/CSV upload): after adoption is non-zero.
- **The CEO's one truth is derived from the ledger and the tap-stored stage.** Nothing on it is
  re-entered or reconciled by hand.
- **Adoption is measured weekly** with one instrument: the Massar activity log shows at least one
  tap by the named rep on each working day, plus the rep's own word at a weekly check-in on whether
  they opened the sheet. Zero taps after two weeks means stop building B beyond slice A; A stays
  live as the product until adoption is non-zero.

### Slice A contract

- **Queue unit:** one row per entity (phone / `accountKey`), aggregating its open opportunity lines
  and its latest tracker outcome. An entity never appears twice.
- **Queue membership:** entities with an open opportunity line at `contact` or `present`, or a
  latest outcome of `interested`, `later`, or `handoff`. Lines at `tech` and beyond belong to the
  board, not the queue, so مؤهل hands the entity off rather than leaving it in place.
- **Queue exit:** a row is hidden until its follow-up date after متابعة, until the next working day
  after تواصلت, and permanently after مؤهل moves it to `tech` or مستبعد closes it.
  `workqueue-domain.ts` takes the last tap timestamp and the follow-up date as inputs, so exit is a
  pure rule.
- **Rank:** seriousness from `signal-domain.ts` descending for entities with ledger turns.
  Ledger-less entities (every sheet import on day one) rank by `daysInStage` descending, then
  follow-up date, then sheet row order as the final tiebreaker. `isLineStalled` is checked first
  but only fires at `tech`/`negotiate` past 14 days, so on day one it is empty unless the sheet's
  stage column maps to those rungs; `daysInStage` is the effective day-one key. For it to mean
  anything, the import maps the sheet's last-contact column («آخر تواصل») to the line's `stage_at`;
  without that every imported line reads zero days and the order collapses to insertion order.
  Seriousness takes over once a prospect has ledger turns.
- **One-tap outcomes**, each written to a store that already exists:

  | Tap | Writes |
  |---|---|
  | تواصلت (contacted) | `events` row, kind `tap:contacted`. Channel-agnostic: an interaction happened; the channel is not recorded until the audit says which channels matter. |
  | متابعة بتاريخ (follow-up) | a `tasks` row with `due_at`, `ref_kind = 'contact'` and the phone (no migration; `tasks.ref_kind` is CHECK-constrained to contact/campaign) + `events` row `tap:followup`. The tracker outcome `later` is written only for ledger-backed entities, because `setOutcome` lazily mints a WhatsApp contact and an imported prospect would then show in campaign views with zero messages. |
  | مؤهل (qualified) | opportunity stage → `tech` + `events` row `tap:qualified` |
  | مستبعد (dropped) | opportunity stage → `lost` + `events` row `tap:dropped` |

  Rules: stage taps never move a line backward (مؤهل on a line already at `negotiate` is a no-op
  with a message). مستبعد always applies because it is the rep's explicit decision, written with
  `created_by` and a note as evidence. Outcome order for the tracker is handoff > interested >
  later; a tap never replaces a stronger held outcome. That guard lives inline in the decline-tap
  handler in `agent.ts` today and `setOutcome` overwrites unconditionally, so the rule is re-homed
  as `canReplaceOutcome(held, tapped)` in `workqueue-domain.ts`, not reused.
  Provenance on every tap is the admin name written into the event note; per-person logins arrive
  with the roles gate.
- **Adoption instrument:** count `tap:*` events per working day. `events` has no actor column and
  slice A runs on one login, so until the roles gate every tap counts as the rep's, and the founder
  does not tap during the pilot.
- **Import:** the rep exports the shared sheet to xlsx/CSV and uploads it through
  `/admin/entities/import`. Phone is the only key. The import is extended to mint one
  `opportunities` row per new entity at stage `contact`, source `other`, `created_by: 'import'`,
  product from the sheet's product column or the default «غير محدد», `stage_at` from the
  last-contact column, and an optional lookup from the sheet's stage column to an `OPP_STAGES` key.
  Re-import is idempotent: a phone that already has any line, open or `lost`, gets no second line,
  so a re-upload never resurrects a rep's مستبعد decision. `createOppLines` hardcodes
  `stage_at = now`, so the import extension adds a `stage_at` parameter to that insert. For the
  pilot, the one-time export counts as acceptable typing.
- **New code, five pieces (effort M):** `src/workqueue-domain.ts` (pure, unit-tested, including
  `canReplaceOutcome`); one dashboard route under ADR-0001; the import extension with its column
  lookup and the `stage_at` parameter; the `tasks` write for follow-ups; the tap endpoints. Tap
  events reuse `db.insertEvent`; everything else is reused.
- **Pilot clock:** starts the day the named rep's 20 prospects are imported.

## Engineering review (2026-09-03) — slice A locked

Reviewed with `/plan-eng-review`; every decision below was put to the founder and accepted.

### Scope decision
- **Entity-only import.** `audience.ts` and `createOppLines` are untouched. Imported rows reach
  the queue as entities; an opportunity line is minted only by a rep's tap.
- **Membership is a product tag.** The queue shows entities carrying the tag the rep picks at the
  top of the screen (`entities.product_tags`, the existing operator marker, applied by the import's
  وسم column or the bulk-tag action), plus contacts a HUMAN filed as `interested`/`later`/`handoff`,
  plus open lines at `contact`/`present`. Agent-filed outcomes do not qualify on their own. The tag
  doubles as the product the مؤهل tap needs.
- **Rank.** Seriousness from `signal-domain.ts` for entities with ledger turns; otherwise days since
  the entity's «آخر تواصل» attribute, parsed by a strict pure `parseSheetDate` (dd/mm/yyyy,
  yyyy-mm-dd, Arabic digits, Excel serial; anything else is null and ranks last under a visible
  «بلا تاريخ» tier); final tiebreak is the entity name, because row order does not survive an
  upsert.
- **Day boundary.** `riyadhDay()` in the domain: Asia/Riyadh, Sunday to Thursday. Used by queue
  exit («next working day»), same-day tap idempotency, and the adoption count.

### Tap contract
- One route, `POST /admin/workqueue/tap`, over a `TAP_KINDS` table (key · Arabic label · writes) in
  the domain. The screen renders from the same table; `scripts/check-workqueue.mjs` parses the
  emitted client script and asserts button keys equal table keys.
- A tap is **one transaction** (`db.applyTap`): event + task + line write commit together or not at
  all, and the route answers 503 `persisted:false` on failure. `insertEvent` stays fire-and-forget
  for the agent; taps use an awaited path.
- Human mint path is **separate from `opp_auto`**, which stays the assistant's ledger. Shared pure
  refusals: product not in the tag registry, test contact. Not shared: the assistant's AI-outcome
  refusal and the deleted-line block; a human tap overrules the model. Idempotency comes from the
  entity's existing line for that product (any stage) plus the same-day repeat rule. مؤهل advances
  an existing open line for the product or mints one at `tech`; مستبعد moves the product's line to
  `lost` with the reason, or mints one at `lost` for a line-less entity, so a re-import can never
  undo the decision.
- Refusals, each a domain decision with a unit test: 404 unknown phone (in neither contacts nor
  entities); 400 `product` / `unknown_product` / `due_at` (past); 409 `stronger_outcome_held`
  (handoff > interested > later); 503 `persisted:false`; 200 `repeated:true` on a same-day identical
  tap with no write.
- Follow-up date is read back from `tasks` (`ref_kind='contact'`, phone), never from an event note.
- Actor is `body.by` (the portal already sends it), default «اللوحة»; the rep's name is set once in
  the portal for the pilot.
- `GET /admin/workqueue` loads entities, opportunities, tasks (Postgres) and contacts (memory) with
  `Promise.all`, returns ranked rows each with a reason string, and carries `connected:boolean`; the
  screen shows «قاعدة البيانات غير متصلة — القائمة ناقصة» and disables taps when false.
- Migration adds `idx_events_phone_ts`.
